Delete your OutOfBook account and data

In the app or on the website: open Settings (the gear), then Account and privacy → Delete my account and data, and confirm. Your account, saved games and repertoire are removed from the server at once, and from that device.

Without the app: go to outofbook.net in any browser, log in with the same Lichess account, then do the same in Settings. If you cannot log in any more, open an issue at github.com/jthunderbird/outofbook/issues with your Lichess username; you will be asked to confirm from that Lichess account, and the data is deleted within 7 days.

What is deleted: your Lichess user id and username, your saved games and your built repertoire. What is kept: engine evaluations of chess positions, which are shared by all users and contain no personal data.

See also the privacy policy.
