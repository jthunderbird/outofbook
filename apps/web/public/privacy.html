Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026. This policy covers OutOfBook at chess.conft.io and the OutOfBook Android apps.

What OutOfBook stores

  • Without logging in, everything stays on your device: the games you import, your repertoire and the engine results are kept in your browser's storage. The server only receives requests for shared engine evaluations of chess positions, which carry no personal data.
  • When you log in with Lichess, the server keeps your Lichess user id and username, the games you choose to save (up to 200, the moves and game details as Lichess or Chess.com publish them) and the repertoire built from them. Engine evaluations of chess positions are shared by all users and are not linked to you.
  • Your Lichess login token stays on your device. The server uses it only to ask Lichess who you are.
  • On the website, an optional Anthropic API key for Claude answers stays in your browser; questions go from your browser straight to Anthropic. The Android apps do not include Claude.

What OutOfBook does not do

  • No ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No passwords: logging in is done on lichess.org.

Other services the app talks to

Your device talks directly to lichess.org (login, importing games, the cloud evaluation of common positions, playing games, sounds) and to chess.com (importing games), under their own privacy policies.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. The server's data is kept in a database on a private server.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account and privacy → Delete my account and data. This removes your account, your saved games and your repertoire from the server at once, and clears them from the device. Without the app, see deleting your account. Logged-out use leaves nothing on the server to delete; clearing the site's data (or uninstalling the app) removes it from your device.

Children

OutOfBook is not directed at children under 13.
